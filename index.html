What We Offer

Everything a teacher needs,
nothing they don't

Built specifically for Indian schools, AttendEase handles the entire student attendance lifecycle — from marking to reporting.

🔐

Secure Teacher Login

Role-based access ensures only authorised teachers can log in and manage their assigned classes. No student data is ever exposed.

✅

One-tap Attendance

Mark every student Present, Absent, or Late in seconds. Bulk-mark an entire class with a single button, or adjust individually.

📤

Excel & CSV Import

Upload your existing student roster from Excel (.xlsx) or CSV files. AttendEase auto-maps columns and skips duplicates intelligently.

📈

Instant Reports

See per-student attendance percentages with colour-coded indicators. Filter by class or date. Export summaries for parents or administration.

👥

Student Management

Add, edit, and remove students. Search by name or roll number. Each profile stores contact details for both student and parent.

📱

Works Everywhere

Fully responsive — works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers. No app install required. Open and go.

How It Works

Up and running in under 3 minutes

No training needed. No complex setup. Just open, log in, and start marking attendance today.

1

Create your teacher account

Sign up in seconds with your school email. Your account is linked to the classes you teach — no one else can see your data.

2

Add or import your students

Type students in manually, or drop your existing Excel roster into the Import page. All data is preserved safely.

3

Take attendance daily

Pick a class and date, mark each student, hit Save. The system accumulates data over time automatically.

4

View reports anytime

Open the Reports page to see per-student summaries, filter by date, and spot students with low attendance instantly.
